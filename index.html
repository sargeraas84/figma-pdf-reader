Everything in one box

Fourteen live tools, all engineered to work offline. Nothing you open ever leaves your device.

📄

Read comfortably

Tabbed documents, continuous scroll, full-text search, fit modes, rotation and night mode — fast on 2 000-page files.

✏️

Edit the text

Click any text run and retype it. Bundled open-licensed fonts (Roboto, Lato, Inter, Merriweather…) are embedded subset into your saved PDF.

🖍️

Annotate

Highlight whole lines or selections, draw freehand with the pen, and manage everything from the annotations sidebar — all baked in on save.

🔒

Redact & protect

Drag real black-out boxes that strip the covered text, and set an open password (AES-256) on any document.

🔄

Convert

Word → PDF, PDF → Word, PDF → PowerPoint with speaker notes, and pages → PNG/JPG images. Two-way, local, no uploads.

🗂️

Organize

Drag pages to reorder them, split a range into a new file, merge several PDFs into one, compress losslessly, run OCR on scans.

Built for the way you read

A dark, focused shell that keeps the page front and center — on desktop, in the browser, and touch-tuned for Android tablets.

🌙

Night mode

One keystroke inverts the page content while keeping the UI dark — easy on the eyes at 2 a.m.

⌨️

Keyboard first

Native menus and accelerators for open, save, find, zoom, fit modes and more.

📱

Tablet ready

Installable PWA plus a sideloadable APK: bigger touch targets, no hover dependency, works offline.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no telemetry, no network calls for the core flows. Your documents stay yours.
